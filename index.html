What we staff

One agency, every role your home or office runs on.

Every placement starts with a written brief from you — hours, days off, language, and the specific tasks — so who we send actually fits.

Maid & housekeeping

Daily or live-in help for cleaning, laundry, dishes and general upkeep, part-time or full-time.

Cook

North/South Indian, regional or diet-specific cooking — matched to how your household actually eats.

Driver

Licence-verified personal or family drivers, for daily commute, school runs or on-call duty.

Elderly care

Companionship, mobility support and daily-routine assistance for ageing parents at home.

Nanny & babysitting

Full-time nannies or evening/weekend babysitting, with childcare experience we verify with references.

Office boy / peon

Pantry, courier runs, filing and general office support for small offices and clinics.
